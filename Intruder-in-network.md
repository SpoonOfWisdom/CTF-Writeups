# Case Study: Attack terms
**Adversary terms**

## Briefing Summary
What is the name of an attack where an attacker intercepts and alters communication between two parties?

## Overview
The term for an attack that intercepts two parties and alters communication/ reads the data sent between them is known as a Man-In-The-Middle attack **MITM**

## Takeaway
An attack that intercepts signals can be dangerous and you need to make sure that the network you are connecting to is the one that you meant to connect to.
