# Case Study: Low severity risk evaluation
**Risk Management**

## Scenario Summary
A low-severity vulnerability is discovered, after weighing all costs, the team decides not to fix it and live with the risk. What is the Type of Risk Management?

## Overview
When there is a low-severity risk as stated in the briefing and the cost of remediation is higher than the impact that could be caused organizations could decide to **accept** the risk; Risk Acceptance.
When managing risk, there are 4 ways of doing so:
1. **Risk Avoidance** Avoids the risk entirely
2. **Risk Transference** Transfers risk to another entity
4. **Risk Mitigation** Prevents risk from happening
5. **Risk Acceptance** Living on and understanding the risk is there

## Takeaway
Accepting risk is a formal and perfectly plausible way to manage it, it's better to sign-off and document the decision rather than ignoring it.
